The accidents

Why these four? Each left behind survivors' accounts and detailed investigation records, and each changed how we think about safety. Air, sea, a snowbound mountain — four places, four eras. In each, you trace what kept people alive.

CH.1United Airlines Flight 232198944 Minutes Without Control
CH.2Hindenburg1937Hotel in the Sky, the Last Landing
CH.3Andrea Doria1956Eleven Hours in the Fog
CH.4Mount Hakkōda1902The March into the Blizzard

About the game

You live through a real accident from the very beginning, through the eyes of one person who was there. Nothing rushes you. You look around, think, and choose what to do. At the end, the History Notes show what really happened — and who survived, and how.

This is not a game made to frighten. It was made with respect for those who died, to learn from the record. Times, figures and quoted words follow the accident reports and other records; the main character and some people and conversations are fictional.

Support, streaming and videos

The game is free to play. Paid versions (BOOTH, Steam) have the same content. If you enjoyed it, picking one up as a small tip toward the next chapter would mean a lot.

Anyone is welcome to stream it or make videos. If your stream or video is monetized, buying the BOOTH or Steam version would be a kind encouragement — but it is entirely optional.

These are real accidents. Please avoid treating them in ways that would hurt the people who died or those connected to them.

Creator

wano, a manga artist in Japan. I love aircraft and accident investigations; working in manufacturing made me fall for the very idea of safety. I wanted a game like this, so I made it.

Contains impacts, fire, and the number of people who died. If you have been through an accident or disaster, please take care of yourself.
